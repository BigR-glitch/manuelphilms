The Filmmaker

A Passion for Moving Pictures

I started with a borrowed handycam at 16 — filming friends, recitals, anything that moved. Seven years and three cameras later, I run a small studio out of Accra building cinematic films for artists, couples and brands across West Africa and beyond.

My work lives in the in-between — the breath before the first note, the look across the aisle, the unplanned laugh. Big productions have their place, but the moments worth keeping usually aren't scripted.
