3. Payment & Cancellation

Deposits & Refunds
  • 50% non-refundable deposit confirms the booking.
  • Remaining balance is due on or before the shoot day.
  • Cancellations made less than 7 days before the shoot forfeit the deposit.
  • Rescheduling is free of charge once, subject to availability.

4. Production & Delivery

Timelines & Revisions
  • Delivery window: 10 – 14 days from the final shoot date.
  • Two rounds of revisions are included in every package.
  • Additional revisions or re-edits are billed hourly.
  • Raw footage is archived for 60 days; long-term archival is available on request.
